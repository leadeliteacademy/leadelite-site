Sound familiar?

Recruiting lives in one person's phone. When they're busy, the pipeline stops.

Most door-to-door companies recruit on hustle and hope. The owner or one star manager brings people in, everyone else waits, and every season starts from zero. The fix isn't more hustle. It's a system every leader on your team runs the same way, every day.

What changes when recruiting is a system
Daily activityLeaders run a Daily Method of Operations and know exactly how many conversations it takes.
The listEveryone builds an Elite 100 List and works their trustlines: hot, warm, lukewarm, then cold.
The askShort, confident piques that open a door without pitching. Referrals when the answer is no.
The handoffEdify a leader, pass the prospect, and let a three-way call do the heavy lifting.
Follow-upEvery prospect has a stage and a next appointment. Nobody slips through the cracks.
