Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed November 18, 2026

Lead Elite Academy, a program of Elite Leadership Consulting Group LLC, wants everyone to be able to use our website, our member portal and our program materials, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, captions or other assistive technology.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. We test our pages with automated tools (axe-core) on phone and desktop sizes, check keyboard navigation, and fix what we find.

What we've done

  • Text and buttons meet WCAG color-contrast requirements.
  • Every page has a "Skip to main content" link and a visible keyboard focus outline.
  • Background video and moving text can be paused with the "Pause motion" button, and they stay paused if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Videos with speech have captions or a transcript.
  • Pages have titles, a language set, and descriptive link and button names.

Known limitations

  • Some downloadable PDFs are not yet fully tagged for screen readers. If you need any document in another format, email us and we'll send an accessible version.
  • Some checkout and form pages are run by third-party software. We've adjusted what we can and report issues to the provider.

Tell us if something doesn't work

If you run into a barrier on our site or in our program, email admin@leadeliteacademy.com with the page and what happened. We aim to reply within 2 business days and to provide the information you need in a format that works for you.
